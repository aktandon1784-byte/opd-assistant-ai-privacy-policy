2. What the app does

OPD Assistant AI is a clinical reference and clinical-decision-support application intended for appropriately qualified healthcare professionals. It provides medical reference information, structured clinical-reference assistance, documentation assistance, calculations, medical-image assistance, and supported document assistance.

The app does not replace professional education, patient examination, clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or applicable clinical guidelines.

3. Information processed

  • User-entered text: questions, clinical descriptions, and other information voluntarily entered into the app.
  • Images: images voluntarily captured or selected for supported medical-image or X-ray features.
  • Documents: supported PDF/document content when the applicable document feature is enabled.
  • Voice input: microphone audio used when the user activates speech recognition. The speech-recognition service may process audio to produce recognized text, which may then be submitted as the user's question.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate the service, such as request/error information and basic service diagnostics where applicable.

4. Medical and patient information

Medical information can be sensitive. Users must only submit information that they are legally, professionally, and institutionally authorized to process.

Users should avoid submitting directly identifying patient information unless it is necessary, authorized, and handled in accordance with applicable confidentiality and privacy requirements.

5. Image and document privacy safeguards

Supported image and document workflows may include privacy-detection and sanitization features designed to identify common identifiers before AI processing. These safeguards reduce privacy risk but cannot guarantee detection of every possible identifier.

Users must review any sanitized image or document before submitting it for AI processing. The original user file is not intentionally overwritten by the privacy-redaction process.

6. How information is used

Information you submit is used to provide the requested clinical-reference, informational, image, or document-related response. Depending on the feature, submitted content may be transmitted from the app to the application's server-side backend and to an AI service provider.

7. AI service provider

The application uses the OpenAI API as an AI service provider. The backend is designed so that the OpenAI API credential is kept server-side and is not embedded in the Android application.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve its models by default unless the customer explicitly opts in. OpenAI also documents retention and abuse-monitoring controls for API usage; those controls can vary by endpoint and configuration.

8. Storage and chat history

The app currently does not require a user account. Chat history is maintained locally for the active app session rather than as a developer-managed cloud account history.

The current application backend code does not intentionally write submitted questions or images to an application database or permanent file store. Operational infrastructure or third-party service logs may still process technical information for security, reliability, abuse prevention, billing, or troubleshooting.

9. Retention and deletion

We aim to minimize retention of user-provided medical content. Temporary sanitized files created during supported privacy workflows are intended to be removed after the relevant operation.

Because the app currently does not provide user accounts, there is no account-based cloud profile to delete. For information under our direct control, privacy or deletion requests may be sent to abhirajdigitalinnovation@gmail.com.

Data processed by third-party providers is subject to the applicable provider's retention and deletion controls and policies.

10. Sharing

Abhiraj Digital Innovation does not sell personal or sensitive user information. User-submitted content may be transferred to service providers necessary to provide requested app functionality, including AI processing, infrastructure, security, and service operation.

11. Security

The production architecture is designed to use encrypted network communication such as HTTPS and to keep API credentials on the server side. Access to sensitive production systems should be limited to authorized personnel and service accounts.

The local development endpoint used during development is not intended for production use.

12. Permissions

The app may request camera, photo/file, or microphone access when the user chooses a feature that requires that capability. These permissions are intended to support the corresponding user-requested feature.

13. Children's privacy

OPD Assistant AI is designed for appropriately qualified healthcare professionals and is not directed to children.

14. Medical disclaimer

AI-generated information may be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Clinically important information must be independently reviewed by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional before use in patient care or other clinical decision-making.

15. Production deployment verification

Before public publication, verify that the production backend uses HTTPS, actual hosting logs/storage behavior are known, OpenAI data controls and retention settings are known, temporary-file cleanup works, and this policy matches the real production data flow.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, backend, service providers, security practices, or legal and platform requirements change. The current version should be published at the privacy-policy URL used in Google Play Console.
